# Governance

ITIN Labs maintains this package. The maintainers review and merge pull requests, decide what goes into a release, and publish it.

Decisions are made in the open, in issues and pull requests. Anyone can propose a change; a maintainer makes the final call, guided by what's described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Breaking changes are discussed in an issue before they're merged.

To reach the maintainers privately, email support@itin.voyage.
